## PAA-style / differentiation questions captured (feed Step 8, not Step 4)

- What's the difference between a semester and a final grade on a high school transcript?
- Does my final grade replace my semester grades, or average them?
- Do colleges look at term/quarter grades or semester grades?
- What is a final transcript and does it show graduation?

Source snippets (Quora/CollegeVine/support-article aggregation, not one of the 4 locked competitors below — used only for the conceptual "what's different" framing, unbolded, per Step 6's "fill a genuine gap" allowance):
- Semester grade = made up of two quarter grades + an exam grade (25–30% exam weight typical for EOC courses).
- Final/course grade = the culminating grade for the full course, sometimes the average of both semester grades when a course spans a full year — transcripts vary by school on whether they show both or just the final.
